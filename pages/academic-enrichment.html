Our Methodology

How We Accelerate Student Growth

A proven 3-phase cycle of diagnostic evaluation, targeted micro-pod instruction, and monthly milestone tracking.

1
Diagnostic Assessment

A 60-minute multi-domain baseline test identifies exact learning gaps, calculation friction points, reading lexile level, and learning style preferences.

2
Customized Learning Pods

Students are matched in 1:4 pods with an accredited educator. Practice modules are calibrated specifically to the student's personal growth roadmap.

3
Monthly Progress Benchmarks

Parents receive monthly milestone reports showing grade-level advancement, percentage mastery gains, and next curriculum goals with complete transparency.
