Non-Negotiable Protocols

Our Four-Pillar Safety Framework

Every policy, door lock, and background check is designed to give parents absolute confidence and peace of mind.

1. 100% DOJ & FBI Vetting

Every tutor, driver, and staff member undergoes Live Scan fingerprinting through the Department of Justice and FBI, identity verification, reference audits, and ongoing background monitoring.

2. Single-Point Secure Entry

All perimeter doors remain electronically locked during operating hours. Single-point reception entry requires digital parent PIN check-in and authorized photo identification.

3. Pediatric First Aid & AED

100% on-site staff maintain current American Heart Association Pediatric First Aid, CPR, and AED certification. Dedicated first aid station and strict EpiPen/allergy protocols.

4. Strict Release Authorization

Students are only released to pre-registered guardians on the parent portal. Any new guardian must present government-issued photo ID matched against parental portal authorization.
