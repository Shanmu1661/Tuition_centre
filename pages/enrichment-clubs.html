Our Signature Clubs

Engaging Passion Projects Every Afternoon

Included at no extra charge in our Core 3-Day and Full 5-Day tuition plans. Facilitated by specialized mentors.

Robotics & Mechanics Grades 2 – 8

LEGO® Spike Prime Robotics

Students build motorized vehicles, robotic arms, and automated sorting machines using ultrasonic sensors, gyros, and smart hubs. Teaches mechanical gear ratios and team debugging.

Days: Mon & Thu (4:30 – 5:30 PM)
Highlight: Monthly Obstacle Course Challenges
Software Studio Grades 3 – 10

Scratch & Python Game Studio

Young developers transition from block-based Scratch games to real Python syntax. Students create playable 2D arcade games, physics simulations, and generative art.

Days: Tue & Fri (4:30 – 5:30 PM)
Highlight: Publishable portfolio games
Strategy & Tactics All Grades

Chess Masters & Strategic Logic

Coached by tournament-rated chess instructors. Students learn opening pawn structures, endgame checkmate patterns, patience, and anticipating opponent moves.

Days: Wed (4:30 – 5:30 PM)
Highlight: End-of-Term Center Tournament
Literacy & Art Grades 2 – 7

Young Authors & Illustrators

Story lovers create their own bound hardback storybooks and comic panels. Covers character design, world building, dialogue, and watercolor illustration.

Days: Mon & Wed (4:30 – 5:30 PM)
Highlight: Bound Published Book to Keep
Science Discovery Grades 1 – 6

Hands-On Science & Chemistry

Safe, fascinating tabletop chemistry and physics experiments: dry ice bubbles, vinegar volcanoes, snap circuit electronics, and plant biology dissection.

Days: Tue & Thu (4:30 – 5:30 PM)
Highlight: Weekly Take-Home Experiments
Fine Arts All Grades

Fine Arts & Clay Sculpting

Air-dry clay modeling, acrylic canvas painting, color theory, and paper sculpture. Provides a calm, meditative artistic outlet after an intense academic day.

Days: Friday Special (4:30 – 5:30 PM)
Highlight: Annual Parent Gallery Night
