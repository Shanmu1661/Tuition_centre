Driving & Free Parking

Located right off the Main Boulevard with immediate access to Learning Lane.

Effortless access for older students arriving via Metro City transit.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions Before Visiting

Got a question before scheduling your campus visit? Here are detailed answers to what parents ask us most frequently.

Yes, we strongly encourage bringing your child! The tour is designed for both of you. While we walk you through our learning methodology, your student will test-drive our ergonomic study pods, try out our interactive digital whiteboards, explore the robotics makerspace, and complete a stress-free 15-minute diagnostic assessment that reveals their natural learning velocity.

During academic business hours (Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM – 6:30 PM), our admissions director typically responds via your preferred contact method (phone, email, or SMS) within 2 hours. If you submit your inquiry over the weekend, our team will reach out by 9:30 AM on Monday morning to confirm your tour reservation.

We offer multiple flexible options for busy working parents:
  • Saturday Morning Open Tours: Available between 10:00 AM and 12:00 PM during our STEM Enrichment Lab.
  • Virtual Evening Video Consultations: 20-minute Google Meet or Zoom consultations hosted on Tuesday and Thursday evenings from 6:30 PM – 8:00 PM.

Student safety is our highest operational commitment:
  • Controlled Access: All perimeter doors remain electronically locked at all times. Visitors must buzz into our front security vestibule and present government-issued photo ID.
  • 100% Vetted Personnel: Every single educator and staff member has cleared mandatory California DOJ & FBI fingerprint clearances and maintains current CPR/First Aid certification.
  • Authorized PIN Dismissal: Students are only released to adults listed on their emergency contact profile using secure multi-factor PIN verification on our front desk tablets.

To help us build the most accurate academic roadmap, we recommend bringing:
  • Your student's most recent report card or quarterly progress report.
  • Sample graded tests, quizzes, or recurring homework assignments where your child experienced difficulty.
  • Any existing IEP, 504 accommodation documentation, or teacher communication notes.
Note: If you don't have these documents available, don't worry! Our initial 15-minute diagnostic will quickly identify your child's baseline concept gaps.
